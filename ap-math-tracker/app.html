Open in the browser

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app above. If the button does not appear, tap the menu ⋮ then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. The AP Math icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
